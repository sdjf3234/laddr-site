Privacy Policy — Laddr

Last updated: 6 November 2026
Contact: support@laddr.cloud

Overview

Laddr is a thin VPN client for Android. It connects your device to a private tunnel using a subscription URL that you paste into the app.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data.

  • Laddr does not create user accounts.
  • Laddr does not collect, store, or sell personal information.
  • Laddr does not run analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • Laddr does not upload connection logs to our servers.

What stays on your device

  • The subscription URL you enter is stored only in local app preferences on your device so you do not have to paste it again.
  • Connection status is shown in the app UI and in a local persistent notification while the VPN is connected.
  • Traffic is sent through the VPN tunnel to the endpoint described by your subscription. We do not inspect or retain that traffic.

Permissions

Laddr requests only the permissions needed to run a VPN service:

  • Internet — to reach your subscription endpoint and the VPN server.
  • VPN / Bind VPN Service — to establish the system VPN tunnel.
  • Foreground service — to keep the tunnel alive with a visible ongoing notification.
  • Notifications — to show connection status while connected (Android 13+).

Third parties

The app uses LibXray / Xray-core on-device to establish the tunnel. Network traffic goes to the server(s) named in your subscription configuration. That server operator’s privacy practices are separate from this app.

Children’s privacy

Laddr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
